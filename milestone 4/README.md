#  Milestone 4 – Revenue Strategy Analysis

## AI-Driven Hotel Revenue Analysis (Power BI)

---

## 📖 Project Overview

Milestone 4 focuses on the development of an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze hotel revenue performance. The dashboard integrates KPIs, time intelligence measures, and multiple analytical visuals to provide actionable insights for hotel management.

This milestone ensures data consistency, accurate calculations (RevPAR, Occupancy %, Revenue Growth), and professional dashboard design.

---

## Objectives

* Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard
* Implement KPI indicators (Revenue, ADR, RevPAR, Occupancy %)
* Apply Time Intelligence for Revenue Growth %
* Analyze revenue across seasons, booking channels, room categories, and guest segments
* Ensure consistent revenue calculations across all visuals

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Revenue** – Combined Room Revenue and Upsell Revenue
* **Upsell Revenue** – Additional income from extra services
* **ADR (Average Daily Rate)** – Average revenue per booked room
* **RevPAR (Revenue per Available Room)**
* **Occupancy %** – Rooms Booked / Rooms Available
* **Revenue Growth %** – Month-over-Month comparison

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visuals

###  KPI Cards

Provides a high-level summary of hotel performance including Total Revenue, ADR, RevPAR, Occupancy %, and Upsell Revenue.

###  Total Revenue by Month 

Displays monthly revenue trends to identify performance fluctuations and seasonal impact.

###  Upsell Revenue by Market Segment 

Shows revenue distribution across different market segments to identify high-value customers.

###  Average ADR by Room Category 

Compares ADR across Premium, Standard, and Budget room categories to support pricing decisions.

###  Revenue by Booking Channel & Hotel Branch 

Analyzes Direct vs OTA performance across hotel branches.

###  ADR & RevPAR by Guest Segment 

Highlights contribution of Returning, Loyal, and First-time guests.

###  Revenue by Season & Room Category

Provides seasonal breakdown (Winter, Summer, Spring) with category-wise revenue analysis.

###  Revenue by Season and Booking Channel 

Compares Direct and OTA revenue performance across seasons.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Winter season generates the highest revenue
* Direct booking channel outperforms OTA
* Premium rooms achieve highest ADR
* Upsell services significantly increase total revenue
* Revenue shows seasonal fluctuations month-over-month
